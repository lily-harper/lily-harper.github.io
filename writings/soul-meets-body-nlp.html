Can Soul Meets Body meet NLP?

I wanted to use NLP to learn about a band, but a band taught me about NLP.

June 2026 View related project

Another limitation can be found in the method I used for sentiment analysis. Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner (VADER) is useful for social media analysis and can even handle punctuation and emojis. VADER uses a rule based dictionary and can even detect intensifies, for example, the word “very”. However, it ignores the context of the words and cannot detect sarcasm or irony as well as a real, feeling person can.

Additionally, Gibbard does not rely on the word “very” for emotionally devastation (very only appears 9 times in the entire corpus). Another example is from Tiny Vessles (transatlanticism) - “that you told her that you love her but you dont” returns a neutral VADER score.

A limitation in the machine learning process I employed is in the violation of its assumption. Ben Gibbard writes songs that belong to multiple themes, which is why I think the variety of interpretations on the r/DeathCabforCutie subreddit is interesting. KMeans assumes that each observation, in this case, each song, belongs to only one cluster, and that that cluster does not overlap with another cluster.

This one reason why I think the evaluation metrics I derived showed less than perfect clusters. (high values for the Davies-Bouldin Index and Inertia Score)
