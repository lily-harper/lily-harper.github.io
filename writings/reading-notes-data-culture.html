Breadcrumbs: Tracing my steps with notes on what I read or saw

June 2026- current

Short notes on readings, art, or research that changed how I think, what I think about, or how I do things. Shared not only because I think these are interesting and would recomend them, but because I want to remember them.

I'll probably come back and expound on these with focus on how it impacted my aims. For now, a basic list will suffice.

Paper

Minimum Wages and Employment: A Case Study of the Fast-Food Industry in New Jersey and Pennsylvania

Card and Krueger. 1994.

WHY I READ IT

I read this for an undergraduate class in labor economics where there was an emphasis on economic research.

Why it stood out to me

This was not only the first difference-in-differences paper I read, but the first quantitative, causal, paper I read. The unintuitive finding was interesting to me.
