Bon Iver Everywhere

Methods

Network analysis Data visualization Graph exploration

Tools

Python Plotly JavaScript
Live visualization

An interactive 3D network for exploring how Bon Iver's collaborations extend across artists, genres, and the wider musical landscape.

Question

How far does Bon Iver's collaborative reach extend, and what paths connect the band to artists beyond its immediate collaborators?

Approach

The visualization represents artists as nodes and musical collaborations as connections. Direct collaborators sit at the center of the analysis, while additional hops reveal how those relationships branch into a much broader network. Artists are grouped by genre to make larger patterns easier to see.

Explore the network

Rotate and zoom through the 3D graph, then select an artist to inspect the tracks they share with Bon Iver or the collaboration path that connects them. The detail panel includes track, role, year, and album information where available.
