Oeuvre

Explications on projects.

I aimed to work on a diverse variety of projects that would develop my data science and statistical skills. I enjoy revisiting projects, but here they are as-is now.

Each project has a repository link with a README.md that addresses how I shaped the project to be thoughtful, purposeful, and reproducible.

  • Thoughtful — I think it is important to be cognizant of the limitations or implications of a piece of work, as well as how a piece of work was created. Additionally, most projects include reflections on the details, meanings of methods, or results (on the 'writings' tab).
  • Purposeful — These projects exist to either understand something, solve a problem, or fill a gap. Sometimes the purpose is personal edification.
  • Reproducible — I aim for anyone to be able to reproduce my projects, either through running the pipeline or from scratch.

All of my projects live on GitHub, which also has additional or in-progress work that is not included in this curated selection.
